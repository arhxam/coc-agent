# Side systems (each is optional; check the matching flags in `coc config`)

## Helpers (Builder's Apprentice, Lab Assistant, Alchemist; if the account has them)
Daily free speed-ups. Assign them every day to the **longest** running upgrade/research. Their work resets
daily, so an unassigned day is lost value.

## Blacksmith and hero equipment (`use_hero_equipment_ore`)
Ore (shiny / glowy / starry) comes from the star bonus, Trader, events and Clan War. Spend it on the equipment
the main army's heroes actually use; don't spread it thin. Equipment upgrades don't use builders.

## Magic items (`use_magic_items`)
Off by default: the owner may want to save them. When on, see principles.md for the best uses. Never buy them with gems unless `use_gems`.

## Trader and free rewards (`collect_rewards`)
Claim free items, season challenge rewards and the **free** track of the Gold Pass. Never buy the Gold Pass,
packs or anything with a real-money price. Gem-priced Trader items only if `use_gems`.

## Clan Games (if in a clan)
Pick tasks that match what you're doing anyway ("loot X gold", "win N battles", "use N spells"). Avoid tasks
that need chat or wars. Claim the rewards at the end.

## Clan Capital (`do_clan_capital`)
On raid weekends, use your raid attacks (they use capital troops, not your army). Collect Capital Gold from the
Forge. Contribute Capital Gold to whatever district the clan is upgrading; don't pick upgrades yourself.

## Donations (`donate_to_clan`)
Fill open requests in the clan panel with troops/spells you already have spare. Tap Donate only, never type
in chat. Don't train extra just to donate unless the profile says so.

## Events
Event challenges and event troops: claim free rewards. Use an event troop only if it helps the farming plan.
