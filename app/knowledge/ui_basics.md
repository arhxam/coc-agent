# UI basics (landscape, typical layout; verify on your device and record exact spots in memory/ui_map.md)

## iPhone Mirroring
- If clicks don't land (an invisible overlay from another app), use the menu bar: **Window → Center** with iPhone Mirroring frontmost.
- If the mirroring window says the iPhone is in use, unlocked or not nearby: the owner has it; finish with `phone_in_use`.

## Home village
- **Top-left:** your name/level, trophies and league; Battle/attack history.
- **Top-middle:** builders `x/y` (tap for the list of running upgrades and suggestions). Helpers / Lab often nearby.
- **Top-right:** gold, elixir, dark elixir, gems (zoom to read; storage caps show when you tap a storage).
- **Bottom-left:** **Attack/Battle** button; army/Quick Train is next to it.
- **Bottom-right:** **Shop** (buildings for gold/elixir in the Defense/Resource/Army tabs; never the Treasure/gem/$ sections).
- Tap a building → the bar at the bottom: Info / Upgrade / (Select row for walls). Upgrade dialog → the confirm
  button must show a gold, elixir or dark elixir icon, **not a gem**.

## Battle
- Scouting: **Available loot** top-left, **Next** bottom-right, timer at the top, troop bar at the bottom.
- **End Battle** bottom-left, then confirm. After the battle: **Return Home** at the bottom center.

## Pop-ups
- Close offers, "special deal" and season pop-ups with the **X** (top-right of the dialog). Never tap a price.
