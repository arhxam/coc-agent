# Upgrade order (general guidance; profiles change the weights)

Use this to write `memory/upgrade_priorities.md` for the actual account. Verify every level in game first:
the game shows what's maxed for the current Town Hall (upgrade button missing or "max level for this TH").

## Right after a Town Hall upgrade (if `allow_new_buildings`)
1. **Build every newly unlocked building** from the Shop (gold/elixir price only): new storages, collectors,
   army camp, defenses. Level 1 is cheap and fast, and storages/camps raise your bank and army size.
2. **Laboratory** upgrade (unlocks the next troop/spell levels) and **Army Camps**.
3. **Clan Castle** (more reinforcement troops = stronger attacks and defense).
4. Then follow the steady-state order below.

## Steady state (max-out default)
1. **Offense:** lab research for the farming army; Spell Factory / Barracks unlocks; siege workshop; heroes
   (dark elixir), one or two at a time.
2. **Key defenses** for this Town Hall (the newest, highest-impact ones the TH unlocked), splash defenses
   (Wizard Tower, Mortar, Bomb Tower), Air Defenses, then point defenses.
3. **Storages and resource buildings** (bigger bank; collectors/drills earn passively).
4. **Traps** and minor buildings.
5. **Walls** on their own track (see principles.md).
6. **Town Hall** when the profile's condition is met (see the profile's direction).

## By Town Hall bracket (what usually matters most)
- **TH2–6:** army camps, lab, Clan Castle; collectors/mines are worth it early. Simple armies farm fine.
- **TH7–9:** heroes arrive (Barbarian King, Archer Queen); dragons and giant-based farming armies get strong.
  Dark elixir drills and storage matter now.
- **TH10–12:** Inferno Towers, Eagle Artillery, siege machines, Grand Warden. Super troops (boosted with dark elixir) make farming much easier.
- **TH13+:** Scattershots, Royal Champion, pets, Blacksmith equipment, and many long upgrades. Builder
  scheduling (long jobs at night) and the lab dominate progress speed. Endgame is walls and equipment.

## Rules of thumb
- Never leave a builder idle to save for the "right" upgrade. Start something.
- Prefer upgrades whose finish time lands when a run is likely (night, idle hours).
- Keep the farming army's key troops and spells ahead of everything else in the lab.
