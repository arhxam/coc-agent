# Walls

_Separate track: surplus only, a few per day._

## How walls work in this game version (verified 2026-11-16)
- Needs an idle builder? **Yes.** With 5/5 busy, Confirm opens the Goblin Builder offer (10 gems): close it with X. The upgrade itself is instant ("Upgrade time: None"), so a free builder can do several walls and then start its real job.
- Multi-select: the wall bar has **Select Row** and **Upgrade More** buttons (not tried yet; try at the next builder free).
- Gold and elixir both accepted: two Upgrade buttons, 1,600,000 gold or 1,600,000 elixir for L13 → L14. Pay in gold (elixir is the bottleneck).
- The blue "Upgrade" button with a number (2) is the **Wall Ring** magic item: never tap.
- How to select: tap a wall segment, e.g. the row at about (700,376) with the window centered; gold Upgrade button at (708,553), Confirm at (782,571).
